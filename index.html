Common questions

  • How do I change the reading speed? Slide the speed row between the picture and the words.
  • How do I record a story? Parent, then Our Family, then choose a person and a story.
  • How do I share recordings with relatives? In Our Family, set up Family Sharing and send them the family code. You approve each person who asks to join.
  • How do I delete my data? Parent, then Privacy and data, then Delete all my data.
