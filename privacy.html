Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-09. Lullivia is a bedtime story app for children, published by DONG EUN KIM. This page explains what the app keeps, what it sends, and how you can delete it.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, and no analytics tools in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your information for marketing.
  • Stories, pictures, lullabies and narration are inside the app. Reading and listening need no account.
  • Family sharing is optional. Only if a grown-up turns it on do any recordings leave the phone.

What stays on your device

These are saved only on your phone or tablet: your settings, favorites and reading progress, the optional child name used for "Good night", the names and photos of family members you add, and voice recordings made on this device.

Removing the app, or using Delete all my data, erases them.

What is stored online when Family Sharing is on

Family Sharing lets relatives record a story in their own voice and lets the family hear it. To do that, the app stores the following on our servers (provided by Supabase, hosted in the United States):

This information is used only to run Family Sharing. It can be seen only by the members the family owner has approved. We do not listen to recordings, use them for advertising, or use them to train AI systems.

  • An anonymous account ID, created automatically when you turn Family Sharing on. There is no email address and no password.
  • The family name you type, and each member's display name and relationship, such as "Mom" or "Grandpa". Use any name you like; if you type a child's first name into the family name, that name is stored with the family and shown to its members.
  • The voice recordings that members choose to send.
  • Family codes and join requests, so a grown-up can approve who joins.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while a grown-up is recording a story, and only after they tap record. Recording needs a grown-up check first.

Children

Lullivia is made for children, but it does not ask a child for any personal information. The settings, Family Sharing and recording areas are behind a grown-up check. The optional child name is used only on the device, unless a grown-up chooses to include it in the family name for Family Sharing.

Other companies

Family Sharing data is stored with Supabase, which acts only as our hosting provider. Apple provides the App Store and TestFlight. The app has no advertising or analytics companies built in.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep Family Sharing data until you delete it.

  • In the app: Parent, then Privacy and data, then Delete all my data. This removes your account and your recordings from our servers, and everything saved on the device. If you are the family owner, the family and all of its shared recordings are removed for every member, so the app asks you to confirm first.
  • A member can leave a family at any time. The family owner can remove a member.
  • If you cannot use the app, write to lulliviastories@gmail.com and we will delete your data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Recordings are kept in private storage with rules that let only approved family members open them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new effective date.
